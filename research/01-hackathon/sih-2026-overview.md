# SIH 2026 Overview

## Context
9th Edition of Smart India Hackathon (SIH). Launched Aug 21, 2026 by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MOE's IC).

## Numbers
- Total problem statements: 226 (172 Software, 54 Hardware)
- DRDO PS: 7 total (4 Software, 3 Hardware)
- Our PS: **SIH26053** (DRDO, Software)

## Timeline (landscape)
- **Idea submission deadline:** 20 Sep 2026
- **National screening:** Oct–Nov 2026 (PPT + demo video + GitHub repo mandatory)
- **Grand Finale:** Dec 2026, 36h hackathon
- All deadlines Asia/Kolkata timezone; verify exact dates on official portal before submission.

## Prize
- ~₹1,00,000 per problem statement (1 winning team per PS). Verify exact amount on catalog page.

## IP Clause
- IP ownership split equally between the problem-statement owner (DRDO) and the winning team, OR as mutually agreed — read the exact wording in the official SIH brochure before signing anything.

- **Do not generate AI content** (video/VO/PPT generation) for the submission package. It risks disqualification and is a promise we keep in `AGENTS.md`.
